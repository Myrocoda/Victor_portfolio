Diabetes Risk Prediction

  • Classification
  • Random Forest
  • ROC-AUC

An end-to-end pipeline on rural patient data: cleaning, exploratory analysis, and a comparison of Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest. A second pass without glucose measures how much the models rely on it.

Random Forest scored best. Dropping glucose cut ROC-AUC from 0.94 to 0.66. With only ~17% diabetic patients, accuracy alone would mislead.

Customer Churn Prediction

  • Random Forest
  • SVM
  • Scikit-learn

Predicts which of 10,000 bank customers will leave, comparing a Random Forest with an RBF-kernel SVM on a stratified 80/20 split with encoded and standardised features.

Accuracy was close (0.865 vs 0.861), but churn recall was low (0.46 vs 0.38). Next steps: class weighting or SMOTE and threshold tuning.

Flower Classification with a CNN

  • Deep learning
  • TensorFlow
  • Streamlit

A convolutional neural network that sorts flower photos into five classes (daisy, dandelion, roses, sunflowers, tulips), with data augmentation and a Streamlit app that returns the predicted class and per-class probabilities.

About 69% validation accuracy. The gap to training accuracy shows overfitting, so transfer learning and early stopping are next.
